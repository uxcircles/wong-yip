Privacy and accessibility

Draft wording for Wong & Yip to review before use.

What this website collects

Nothing. The forms on this site do not send anything to a server: they open WhatsApp or your email app with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. The calculators run in your browser. There are no analytics or advertising cookies. The map loads tiles from OpenFreeMap when you scroll to it.

When you contact us

We use what you send us to answer you and, if you become a client, to provide our services and meet our legal duties, including anti-money laundering checks and keeping records for HMRC. We keep client records for at least six years. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing info@wongyipaccountants.co.uk.

Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion and screen reader labels. If something does not work for you, call or email us and we will help.
