Services

Ten services in three groups, the same way our clients arrive: with a personal tax question, a business to run, or a property to buy or let.

For you

Personal tax management

The UK taxes residents on worldwide income, so rent from a flat in Hong Kong belongs on your UK return. We look after your HMRC personal tax account, tax codes and refunds, and file for you if you live overseas and cannot use HMRC online services.

Self Assessment

Self-employed, landlords, partners, company directors and anyone with income HMRC has not already taxed. We prepare and file your SA100, work out what is due by 31 January, and check whether Making Tax Digital applies to you.

Cross-border tax, UK and Hong Kong

Residence under the Statutory Residence Test, double tax relief between the UK and Hong Kong, and accounting around a move to the UK.

For your business

UK business set-up

Choosing between sole trader, limited company and LLP; registering with Companies House; company secretarial work, registered office and confirmation statements.

Bookkeeping and accounts

Bookkeeping, management accounts and year-end financial statements under UK GAAP or IFRS.

Corporation Tax

CT600 returns and the reliefs that reduce the bill: capital allowances, the Annual Investment Allowance, losses, R&D and creative sector reliefs. Main rate 25%, small profits rate 19% up to £50,000.

Payroll and PAYE

Registering as an employer, running payroll, RTI submissions, payslips, P60s and workplace pensions, so tax reaches HMRC by the 22nd.

VAT

Registration once taxable turnover passes £90,000, quarterly returns under Making Tax Digital, VAT planning and HMRC enquiries.

Outsourced accounting

Your finance team without the hiring: billed by the hour, monthly or per service, or a mix.

E-commerce accounting

Amazon, eBay, Shopify and TikTok Shop sellers: marketplace VAT, multi-currency payouts and stock.

For your property

Stamp Duty analysis and refunds

What you will pay before you offer, whether first-time buyer relief applies to you, and claims to get the 2% non-resident surcharge or the 5% higher rates back.

Overseas landlords

Registering for the Non-Resident Landlord Scheme, rental accounts, allowable expenses and losses, and your annual return to HMRC from abroad.
